Microphone off · loading instructions

Before we begin

Use the Mac microphone in a quiet room. Stay at your normal speaking distance, stop other audio and mute other microphone tests. Paprika will not speak during this check.

Each turn records eight seconds of new audio, including nearby sounds. The microphone turns off before you review. Clips stay in this page and are processed on this Mac; you choose whether they also go to OpenAI.

Where may these new clips be processed?

Results appear as A and B until you finish reviewing, so you can judge the words first. A test outcome is never marked successful for you.

Privacy, cost and what this measures

Cloud processing uses OpenAI’s audio transcription endpoint. This adult-only test does not establish child-data readiness or Zero Data Retention configuration. Each test makes at most four cloud requests, with at most $0.20 conservatively reserved in the existing $25 ledger. API failures are not automatically retried.

This compares words and processing time after a complete recording. It does not measure wake reliability, interruption, speaker identity or complete conversational response delay. Local audio-inclusive saves go to the private project results folder; no saved recording is publicly served. Closing or reloading before Save loses browser-held clips.
