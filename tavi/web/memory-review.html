What is this review for?

We are choosing how Paprika phrases remembered facts. These six replies came from a test with fictional people. Dates and uncertainty are deliberately explicit. The wording can be cautious or formal; we need your judgment about that tradeoff.

This does not choose a memory engine, approve factual mistakes, enroll a voice, or activate memory in the toy. Reading cannot establish how the final voice will feel. Your ratings and optional note are saved locally on the external drive. Nothing is sent to a cloud service.
